# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the remote work policy for all Acme Corp employees effective January 2026.

## Eligibility
- Full-time employees who have completed 6 months of service
- Roles that do not require physical presence
- Employees with a performance rating of "meets expectations" or above

## Working Hours
- Core hours: 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM (local time)
- Employees must be reachable via Slack and email during core hours
- Total working hours: minimum 8 hours per day

## Equipment
- Company will provide: laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse
- Employee is responsible for: internet connection, desk, chair
- IT support is available Monday–Friday 9 AM – 6 PM

## Security
- VPN must be used when accessing company resources
- Screens must be locked when stepping away
- Public Wi-Fi is prohibited for company work without VPN

## Leave and Attendance
- Remote employees follow the same leave policy as office employees
- Attendance is tracked via project management tools
- Unplanned absences must be reported to manager within 1 hour

## Violations
Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
